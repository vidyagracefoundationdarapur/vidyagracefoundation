Our Mission

Vidya Grace Foundation exists to empower the students of Darapur (Moga) Punjab through education, sports, recognition, and opportunity.

We are committed to supporting academic excellence by providing scholarships, celebrating student achievements, and fostering a culture that values learning, integrity, and community growth.

Through transparent and community-centered initiatives, we aim to remove barriers to education and create pathways for every child in Darapur to realize their full potential.
